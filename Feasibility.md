### **Slide 3: Technical Feasibility**

- **Strengths:**
    - Cross-platform development using **Flutter**.
    - Scalable backend with **Node.js + Django**.
    - AI-powered recommendations and chatbot.
    - AR visualization for product previews.
    - Integration with payment gateways and courier services.
- **Challenges:**
    - AI & AR implementation complexity.
    - Database management between **MongoDB & MySQL**.
    - Real-time chat and dynamic pricing require WebSockets.
- **Suggested Improvements:**
    - Start with AI chatbot & recommendation system, add AR later.
    - Consider **PostgreSQL** instead of MongoDB & MySQL.
    - Use **Firebase Firestore** for real-time messaging.

---

### **Slide 4: Financial Feasibility**

- **Strengths:**
    - Multiple monetization strategies: **commissions, subscriptions, ads**.
    - Low initial costs (development, hosting, marketing).
    - Subscriptions create stable revenue.
- **Challenges:**
    - Revenue depends on seller growth.
    - AI & AR development costs can be high.
    - Customer acquisition and marketing expenses.
- **Suggested Improvements:**
    - **Freemium model for sellers:** Free basic listings, premium visibility options.
    - **Launch with manual recommendations** before AI automation.
    - **Start with commission-based revenue**, add ads and subscriptions later.

---

### **Slide 5: Market Feasibility**

- **Strengths:**
    - Growing demand for **handmade & sustainable products**.
    - Large potential user base (artisans, home chefs, custom tailors, etc.).
    - Supports digital inclusion with **multi-language accessibility**.
- **Challenges:**
    - Competes with Etsy, Shopify, and Instagram Shops.
    - Convincing sellers to join and actively use the platform.
    - Balancing supply (sellers) and demand (buyers).
- **Suggested Improvements:**
    - **No-commission model for first few months** to attract sellers.
    - **Localized marketing focus**, expand later.
    - **Social media integration** for seller outreach.

---

### **Slide 6: Operational Feasibility**

- **Strengths:**
    - Automated order tracking & payments.

    - Event planning & group orders.
- **Challenges:**
    - Handling **customer disputes & refunds**.
    - Dependence on third-party courier services.
    - Ensuring **product quality control**.
- **Suggested Improvements:**
    - **Seller verification process** to filter unreliable vendors.
    - **Seller training workshops** for better user experience.
    - **Escrow payment system** to secure transactions.
