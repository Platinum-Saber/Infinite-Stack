## Arduino Board Overview

Arduino is an open-source electronics platform that consists of microcontroller boards and an easy-to-use development environment (Arduino IDE). It is widely used for embedded systems, robotics, automation, and IoT projects.

### Common Arduino Boards & Specifications

#### 1. **Arduino Uno (R3)**

- **Microcontroller:** ATmega328P
- **Clock Speed:** 16 MHz
- **Digital I/O Pins:** 14 (6 PWM)
- **Analog Input Pins:** 6
- **Flash Memory:** 32 KB
- **SRAM:** 2 KB
- **EEPROM:** 1 KB
- **Operating Voltage:** 5V
- **Power Supply:** 7-12V (recommended)

#### 2. **Arduino Mega 2560**

- **Microcontroller:** ATmega2560
- **Clock Speed:** 16 MHz
- **Digital I/O Pins:** 54 (15 PWM)
- **Analog Input Pins:** 16
- **Flash Memory:** 256 KB
- **SRAM:** 8 KB
- **EEPROM:** 4 KB
- **Operating Voltage:** 5V
- **Power Supply:** 7-12V (recommended)

#### 3. **Arduino Nano**

- **Microcontroller:** ATmega328P
- **Clock Speed:** 16 MHz
- **Digital I/O Pins:** 14 (6 PWM)
- **Analog Input Pins:** 8
- **Flash Memory:** 32 KB
- **SRAM:** 2 KB
- **EEPROM:** 1 KB
- **Operating Voltage:** 5V
- **Power Supply:** 7-12V (recommended)
