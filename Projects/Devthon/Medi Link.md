### 1. Project Overview

Our project aims to develop a web application that connects patients with nearby pharmacies, allowing them to upload prescriptions and receive responses regarding medicine availability and price estimates. The platform will utilize geo-location services to display nearby pharmacies, facilitate online payments, and integrate third-party delivery services to streamline medicine procurement. Additionally, it will offer a subscription-based medicine delivery service for patients with chronic conditions, ensuring they receive their medications periodically without the need for repeated orders.

---

### 2. Problem Statement

Patients often struggle to find nearby pharmacies that stock their required medicines, leading to unnecessary delays and inconvenience. Additionally, verifying the price before visiting a pharmacy can be difficult. For patients with chronic conditions, maintaining a consistent supply of medication is crucial, yet it is often disrupted due to missed refills. Our solution aims to bridge this gap by providing a platform where patients can upload their prescriptions, receive real-time responses from pharmacies, and automate medicine deliveries.

---

### 3. Proposed Solution

The proposed solution is a web application that allows users to:

- Upload prescriptions securely.
- Receive availability confirmation and price estimates from nearby pharmacies.
- Make payments through an integrated payment portal.
- Opt for third-party delivery services to receive medicines at their doorstep.
- Subscribe to periodic medicine delivery for chronic conditions.
- Utilize predictive analytics for pharmacies to manage stock based on subscription trends.

---

### 4. Key Features

- **User-friendly interface** for easy prescription uploads and navigation.
- **Geo-location services** to display nearby pharmacies.
- **Real-time responses** from pharmacies regarding medicine availability and pricing.
- **Secure payment gateway** for hassle-free transactions.
- **Integration with third-party delivery services** for medicine dispatch.
- **Chat feature** for direct communication between patients and pharmacists.
- **Order tracking** to monitor the delivery status.
- **Subscription-based medicine delivery** for patients with chronic conditions.
- **Predictive analytics** for pharmacies to optimize stock levels and predict disease patterns based on medicine consumption trends.
