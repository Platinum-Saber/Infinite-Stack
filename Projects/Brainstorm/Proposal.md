

### **NutriMithu – AI-Powered Nutrition App for Sri Lankan Lifestyles**

**Overview:**

NutriMithu is an AI-integrated mobile application focused on personalized diet and nutrition planning tailored specifically for Sri Lankan users. The app combines culturally relevant food databases with intelligent recommendations to promote healthier living through familiar dietary patterns.

**Core Features:**

- **Localized Meal Database:** Includes a wide variety of Sri Lankan foods with estimated nutritional data, aiding users in accurate meal tracking.
    
- **Personalized Diet Planning:** Dynamically generates diet plans based on user inputs like age, weight, goals, and dietary preferences.
    
- **AI Health Assistant:** Integrated NVIDIA-powered chatbot offers real-time, context-aware answers on nutrition, diets, and healthy eating habits.
    
- **Smart Recommendations:** Plans are refined using a vector database (planned) for context-specific suggestions, particularly around local food habits.
    
- **Firebase Integration:** Secure authentication and cloud data storage via Firebase Authentication and Firestore.
    
- **Modular Flutter Frontend:** Built with reusable components for consistency, maintainability, and rapid development.
    

**Vision:**

NutriMithu aims to bridge the gap between AI-driven nutrition science and regional dietary practices. With deep integration of local cuisine, intelligent guidance, and user-centered design, the app seeks to empower Sri Lankans to make informed and sustainable health decisions.
