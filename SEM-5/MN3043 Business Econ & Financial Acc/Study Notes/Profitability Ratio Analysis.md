

# Profitability Ratio Analysis of People’s Leasing & Finance PLC (2023 vs 2024)

## Introduction

Profitability ratios are financial metrics that assess a company’s ability to generate earnings relative to sales, assets, and equity[investopedia.com](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/p/profitabilityratios.asp#:~:text=A%20profitability%20ratio%20is%20a,its%20assets%2C%20equity%2C%20and%20sales). These ratios provide insight into the financial performance and efficiency of a business, indicating how well management converts revenues into profit[investopedia.com](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/p/profitabilityratios.asp#:~:text=Key%20Takeaways). Higher profitability ratios generally signal better performance, as the company is more effective at controlling costs and utilizing resources to produce profit[investopedia.com](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/p/profitabilityratios.asp#:~:text=,industry%2C%20or%20the%20industry%20average). In this report, we analyze key profitability ratios for People’s Leasing & Finance PLC (Company-only, not consolidated) for the financial years ending March 31, 2023 and March 31, 2024. The ratios examined are **Gross Profit Margin**, **Operating Profit Margin**, **Net Profit Margin**, **Return on Assets (ROA)**, and **Return on Equity (ROE)**. For each ratio, we present the formula, identify which financial statements provide the input data, calculate the values for 2023 and 2024 using figures from the company’s financial statements, and interpret the year-over-year trend. All monetary figures are in Sri Lankan Rupees (Rs. '000) as reported in the Statements of Profit or Loss and Financial Position of People’s Leasing & Finance PLC (Company).

## Gross Profit Margin

**Definition & Importance:** Gross Profit Margin measures the proportion of revenue that remains after accounting for the cost of goods sold (or direct costs of generating revenue). It is a margin ratio indicating how efficiently a company produces its goods or services relative to its revenue[investopedia.com](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/p/profitabilityratios.asp#:~:text=Gross%20profit%20margin%2C%20also%20known,compares%20gross%20profit%20to%20revenue). A higher gross profit margin means the company retains more profit from each rupee of revenue to cover other operating costs and generate earnings[investopedia.com](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/p/profitabilityratios.asp#:~:text=A%20company%20with%20a%20high,may%20point%20to%20increased%20competition). This ratio is particularly important as a first indicator of profitability – it reflects pricing power and cost control at the most fundamental level of production or service delivery.

 

**Formula:** Gross Profit Margin = (Gross Profit / Revenue) × 100%. Gross Profit is calculated as total revenue minus direct costs (COGS or, for a finance company, interest expense and similar direct costs)[365financialanalyst.com](https://365financialanalyst.com/knowledge-hub/financial-analysis/profitability-ratios/#:~:text=Gross~profit~margin%3D%5Cfrac%20%7BRevenue). In a finance company like People’s Leasing & Finance, **Gross Profit** can be taken as the “Net income after interest expense,” i.e. total operating income (interest and other operating income minus interest expense), while **Revenue** corresponds to total gross income.

 

**Financial Statement Sources:** For People’s Leasing & Finance PLC, the relevant figures come from the **Statement of Profit or Loss**. _Revenue_ (or **Gross Income**) is the total income earned (including interest, fees, and other income), and _direct cost_ is primarily **Interest Expense** (the cost of funds). The Gross Profit is essentially **Gross Income – Interest Expense**, which is reported as **Total Operating Income** in the income statement.

 

**Calculation (2023 vs 2024):** In the year 2024, the company’s Gross Income was Rs. 31,036,624k and interest expense was Rs. 17,200,885k. The difference is a gross profit (total operating income) of Rs. 13,835,739k for 2024. For 2023, Gross Income was Rs. 32,018,308k with interest expense Rs. 18,206,943k, yielding a gross profit of Rs. 13,811,365k. Using the formula, the **Gross Profit Margin** is approximately **43.1%** for 2023 and **44.6%** for 2024 (gross profit ÷ revenue × 100). This slight increase in margin from 2023 to 2024 indicates that the company earned a bit more gross profit from each rupee of revenue in 2024 compared to the previous year.

 

_Figure: Gross Profit Margin (% of revenue) for 2023 and 2024._

 

As shown in the figure above, People’s Leasing & Finance PLC’s gross profit margin edged up from about **43.1% in 2023** to **44.6% in 2024**. This upward trend suggests a modest improvement in the company’s cost efficiency at the gross level. In 2024, despite a slight drop in total income (revenue) by 3.1%, interest costs declined by a larger percentage (interest expense fell ~5.5% year-over-year). The result was that the company retained a higher portion of its income as gross profit. A higher gross margin indicates that People’s Leasing & Finance was slightly more effective in managing its direct costs (primarily the cost of funds) relative to income in 2024. This positive development means more income was available to cover operating expenses and contribute to net profit, reflecting improved pricing or funding cost management compared to 2023.

## Operating Profit Margin

**Definition & Importance:** Operating Profit Margin is the percentage of revenue remaining after covering **all operating expenses** (direct costs and operating overheads). It is calculated as operating profit (earnings before interest and taxes, in a non-financial context) divided by revenue[investopedia.com](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/p/profitabilityratios.asp#:~:text=Operating%20margin%20is%20the%20percentage,compares%20operating%20profit%20to%20revenue). This ratio shows how efficiently a company’s core business operations generate profit before considering financing costs and taxes. A higher operating margin means the company is better at controlling its operating expenses relative to its revenue, indicating efficient management and cost control in operations[investopedia.com](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/p/profitabilityratios.asp#:~:text=Operating%20margin%20can%20indicate%20how,costs%20down%20and%20maximize%20profitability). For a finance company, operating profit margin reflects profitability after accounting for interest expense, loan impairment provisions, and operating expenses such as personnel and administration costs.

 

**Formula:** Operating Profit Margin = (Operating Profit / Revenue) × 100%. Here, **Operating Profit** is profit after all operating expenses have been deducted from gross profit (it is equivalent to EBIT in many contexts)[365financialanalyst.com](https://365financialanalyst.com/knowledge-hub/financial-analysis/profitability-ratios/#:~:text=The%20operating%20profit%20margin%E2%80%94earnings%20before,ratio%20using%20the%20following%20formula). **Revenue** is total income (gross income). In People’s Leasing & Finance’s financials, Operating Profit can be identified as the “Operating profit before taxes on financial services,” which is profit from operations prior to any taxes (financial services VAT or income tax).

 

**Financial Statement Sources:** We obtain operating profit and revenue figures from the **Statement of Profit or Loss**. Operating profit is given directly in the income statement (after deducting personnel costs, depreciation, impairment charges, and other operating expenses from the total operating income). The revenue (gross income) is the same figure used earlier from the P&L.

 

**Calculation (2023 vs 2024):** For 2024, People’s Leasing & Finance PLC’s standalone **Operating Profit** was Rs. 7,296,856k, and for 2023 it was Rs. 5,973,224k. These figures represent operating profit before deducting taxes. Using the same revenue (gross income) values as before (Rs. 31,036,624k in 2024 and Rs. 32,018,308k in 2023), the **Operating Profit Margin** works out to approximately **23.5%** in 2024, up from about **18.7%** in 2023.

 

_Figure: Operating Profit Margin (% of revenue) for 2023 and 2024._

 

The chart above illustrates that the operating profit margin improved significantly year-over-year – rising from **18.7% in 2023** to **23.5% in 2024**. This indicates that in 2024 the company converted a much larger share of its revenue into operating profit compared to 2023. The jump in operating margin can be attributed to both **increased operating income** and **effective cost management**. Notably, the 2024 financials show a substantial favorable swing in impairment charges: in 2024 the company recorded a net reversal of impairment (a credit of Rs. 1.16 billion) whereas 2023 had an impairment charge of Rs. 568 million. This reversal effectively boosted the 2024 operating profit. Even though certain operating expenses (like other operating expenses) rose in 2024, the overall operating income grew and expenses were controlled well enough that operating profit increased by 22%. The higher operating margin in 2024 suggests improved operational efficiency – the company was better at managing operating costs (including loan loss provisions and overheads) relative to its income, leading to a stronger core profitability.

## Net Profit Margin

**Definition & Importance:** Net Profit Margin (also called **Net Margin**) is the percentage of revenue that remains as **net profit** after **all expenses** have been deducted, including interest and taxes[investopedia.com](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/p/profitabilityratios.asp#:~:text=The%20gross%20profit%20margin%2C%20operating,reflects%20the%20profit%20from%20the)[investopedia.com](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/p/profitabilityratios.asp#:~:text=Net%20Profit%20Margin). It essentially shows how much of each rupee of revenue the company ultimately keeps as earnings. This is a comprehensive indicator of overall profitability and cost control – a higher net profit margin means the company is more successful at converting revenue into actual profit after covering all costs. It reflects not only operational efficiency but also the impact of financing costs and tax management. Investors and analysts often view net profit margin as a key measure of a firm’s overall financial health[investopedia.com](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/p/profitabilityratios.asp#:~:text=Net%20profit%20margin%20is%20seen,keeping%20all%20costs%20under%20control).

 

**Formula:** Net Profit Margin = (Net Profit / Revenue) × 100%. **Net Profit** (net income) is the profit after all expenses and taxes have been subtracted from revenue[365financialanalyst.com](https://365financialanalyst.com/knowledge-hub/financial-analysis/profitability-ratios/#:~:text=). **Revenue** is total income (gross revenue).

 

**Financial Statement Sources:** For calculation, we use the **Statement of Profit or Loss**. _Net Profit_ is the “Profit for the year” attributable to equity holders of the company (since we are considering the company-only figures) and _Revenue_ is again the gross income from the P&L. The profit for the year is found at the bottom of the income statement, after taxes.

 

**Calculation (2023 vs 2024):** In 2024, People’s Leasing & Finance’s **Net Profit (Company)** was Rs. 3,540,497k, compared to Rs. 3,017,638k in 2023. Using the revenue figures (Rs. 31,036,624k in 2024 and Rs. 32,018,308k in 2023), the **Net Profit Margin** is approximately **11.4%** for 2024 versus **9.4%** for 2023.

 

_Figure: Net Profit Margin (% of revenue) for 2023 and 2024._

 

The net profit margin for People’s Leasing & Finance PLC increased from **9.4% in 2023** to **11.4% in 2024**, as depicted in the figure. This indicates that the company was able to convert a larger portion of its revenue into actual profit in 2024. The rise in net margin aligns with the improvements observed in the gross and operating margins – it signifies that not only did operating performance improve, but the company also managed its interest costs and taxes such that more of the revenue flowed through to the bottom line. In 2024, profit after tax grew by about 17% year-on-year while revenue slightly declined, resulting in a higher net margin. This improvement suggests enhanced overall efficiency and profitability. It may be attributed to factors such as reduced funding costs, effective cost containment, and the one-time gain from impairment reversals, coupled with relatively stable tax expenses (income tax expense did increase in 2024, but the effect was outweighed by the higher pre-tax profit growth). A higher net profit margin in 2024 is a positive sign, indicating stronger overall financial performance and better returns on each unit of revenue compared to 2023.

## Return on Assets (ROA)

**Definition & Importance:** Return on Assets (ROA) measures how effectively a company uses its assets to generate profit. It is the ratio of net profit to total assets, often expressed as a percentage[investopedia.com](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/p/profitabilityratios.asp#:~:text=Profitability%20is%20assessed%20relative%20to,income%20divided%20by%20total%20assets). ROA indicates the profit earned per rupee of assets and thus reflects how efficiently management is deploying the company’s asset base to produce earnings[365financialanalyst.com](https://365financialanalyst.com/knowledge-hub/financial-analysis/profitability-ratios/#:~:text=Return%20on%20assets%20,the%20company%20deploys%20its%20capital). A higher ROA means the company is generating more profit from its assets, signifying efficient use of assets. This ratio is especially relevant for financial institutions like People’s Leasing & Finance, which have large asset bases; it helps investors gauge how well the company’s lending and investment assets are yielding returns.

 

**Formula:** ROA = (Net Profit / Total Assets) × 100%. Typically, net profit is compared to average total assets over the period[365financialanalyst.com](https://365financialanalyst.com/knowledge-hub/financial-analysis/profitability-ratios/#:~:text=We%20use%20the%20following%20formula,calculate%20this%20measure%20of%20profitability). In practice, if average assets are not readily available, year-end total assets can be used as an approximation for a quick analysis. **Net Profit** is the net income for the year (from the income statement), and **Total Assets** is the sum of all assets at the end of the year (from the Statement of Financial Position).

 

**Financial Statement Sources:** We take _Net Profit_ from the **Statement of Profit or Loss** (profit for the year) and _Total Assets_ from the **Statement of Financial Position** (the total assets reported as of 31 March of each year). Here we use the company’s standalone total assets (excluding any group consolidation effects) as given in the balance sheet.

 

**Calculation (2023 vs 2024):** As noted, the company’s net profits were Rs. 3,017,638k in 2023 and Rs. 3,540,497k in 2024. The total assets of the company stood at Rs. 171,073,162k as of 31 March 2023 and Rs. 159,852,088k as of 31 March 2024. Using the year-end asset figures for a simple ROA estimate, **ROA (2023)** = 3,017,638 / 171,073,162 × 100% ≈ **1.76%**, and **ROA (2024)** = 3,540,497 / 159,852,088 × 100% ≈ **2.21%**.

 

_Figure: Return on Assets (ROA) for 2023 and 2024._

 

The ROA for People’s Leasing & Finance PLC improved from about **1.8% in 2023** to **2.2% in 2024**, as illustrated above. This upward movement indicates that the company generated more profit per rupee of assets in 2024 than it did in the previous year. Several factors underlie this improvement: First, **net earnings increased** by 17% in 2024, reflecting higher profitability. Second, the **asset base actually contracted** by roughly 6.6% (total assets fell from ~Rs.171 billion to ~Rs.160 billion), due in part to reduced lending portfolios or other asset adjustments. When a company earns higher profit on a slightly smaller asset base, its ROA naturally rises. The higher ROA in 2024 suggests better efficiency in asset utilization – the assets of People’s Leasing & Finance were put to more productive use, yielding greater returns. This could be due to improved interest spreads, better asset quality (leading to lower impairments), or a more optimal mix of earning assets. In summary, the company’s profitability relative to its assets strengthened in 2024, a positive sign that indicates improved management of assets or operating environment compared to 2023.

## Return on Equity (ROE)

**Definition & Importance:** Return on Equity (ROE) measures the return generated on the owners’ investment in the company. It is defined as net profit divided by shareholders’ equity[investopedia.com](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/p/profitabilityratios.asp#:~:text=Return%20on%20Equity%20). ROE indicates how effectively a company is using shareholders’ funds to create profits. For shareholders, ROE is a critical metric, as it shows the percentage return on each rupee of equity invested in the company[365financialanalyst.com](https://365financialanalyst.com/knowledge-hub/financial-analysis/profitability-ratios/#:~:text=Return%20on%20equity%20,important%20and%20tracked%20profitability%20ratios). A higher ROE means the company is more efficient at converting the equity capital into net income – essentially, it signifies stronger profitability for the owners. It’s often used to compare profitability across companies, and to track a company’s performance over time in generating value for shareholders.

 

**Formula:** ROE = (Net Profit / Shareholders’ Equity) × 100%. Net Profit is the profit after tax (from the income statement) and Shareholders’ Equity is the total equity attributable to the company’s shareholders (from the balance sheet). Like ROA, the net profit is often compared to average equity over the period[365financialanalyst.com](https://365financialanalyst.com/knowledge-hub/financial-analysis/profitability-ratios/#:~:text=Return%20on%20equity%20,important%20and%20tracked%20profitability%20ratios), but here we will use the year-end equity for consistency with available data.

 

**Financial Statement Sources:** The inputs are drawn from two statements: _Net Profit_ from the **Statement of Profit or Loss** (company profit for the year) and _Total Shareholders’ Equity_ from the **Statement of Financial Position** (total equity attributable to equity holders at year-end). We consider only the company’s equity (excluding non-controlling interests, which are only relevant at the group level – in the company-only financials, there is no NCI).

 

**Calculation (2023 vs 2024):** People’s Leasing & Finance PLC’s standalone profit for 2023 was Rs. 3,017,638k and for 2024 was Rs. 3,540,497k. The corresponding total equity (Company’s shareholders’ equity) was Rs. 39,792,065k as of 31 March 2023 and Rs. 41,826,138k as of 31 March 2024. Using these figures, **ROE (2023)** = 3,017,638 / 39,792,065 × 100% ≈ **7.58%**, and **ROE (2024)** = 3,540,497 / 41,826,138 × 100% ≈ **8.46%**.

 

_Figure: Return on Equity (ROE) for 2023 and 2024._

 

The ROE of People’s Leasing & Finance PLC increased from roughly **7.6% in 2023** to **8.5% in 2024**, as shown in the chart. This means that in 2024 the company generated about 8.5 cents of profit for every rupee of equity, compared to about 7.6 cents per rupee in the prior year. The rise in ROE reflects the company’s enhanced ability to deliver returns to its shareholders. Contributing to this improvement was the growth in net profit (17% higher in 2024 than 2023) coupled with only a modest increase in equity (shareholders’ equity grew by about 5% year-over-year due to retained earnings and reserves). Because profit grew at a faster rate than equity, the return on equity rose. This trend is favorable for shareholders, as it indicates that the company is not sitting on underutilized capital; instead, it is leveraging its equity base to produce higher earnings. A ROE of 8.5% (while an improvement) is moderate, suggesting there is still room to further enhance shareholder returns, but the year-over-year gain signals a positive trajectory in how effectively the company is employing equity capital to generate profits.

## Conclusion

In summary, the profitability ratios of People’s Leasing & Finance PLC (Company-only) show a **clear improvement from 2023 to 2024**. Overall, the year-over-year trend points to a strengthening profitability profile for People’s Leasing & Finance PLC in 2024. The company managed to boost profits despite a slight dip in revenue, implying success in cost management and perhaps more prudent risk/asset management (as evidenced by the reduction in impairment charges). These improvements in profitability ratios signal that the company’s financial performance and efficiency have improved, which is a positive sign for stakeholders. For a finance company, operating profit margin reflects profitability after accounting for interest expense, loan impairment provisions, and operating expenses such as personnel and administration costs.

 

**Formula:** Operating Profit Margin = (Operating Profit / Revenue) × 100%. Here, **Operating Profit** is profit after all operating expenses have been deducted from gross profit (it is equivalent to EBIT in many contexts)[365financialanalyst.com](https://365financialanalyst.com/knowledge-hub/financial-analysis/profitability-ratios/#:~:text=The%20operating%20profit%20margin%E2%80%94earnings%20before,ratio%20using%20the%20following%20formula). **Revenue** is total income (gross income). In People’s Leasing & Finance’s financials, Operating Profit can be identified as the “Operating profit before taxes on financial services,” which is profit from operations prior to any taxes (financial services VAT or income tax).

 

**Financial Statement Sources:** We obtain operating profit and revenue figures from the **Statement of Profit or Loss**. Operating profit is given directly in the income statement (after deducting personnel costs, depreciation, impairment charges, and other operating expenses from the total operating income). The revenue (gross income) is the same figure used earlier from the P&L.

 

**Calculation (2023 vs 2024):** For 2024, People’s Leasing & Finance PLC’s standalone **Operating Profit** was Rs. 7,296,856k, and for 2023 it was Rs. 5,973,224k. These figures represent operating profit before deducting taxes. Using the same revenue (gross income) values as before (Rs. 31,036,624k in 2024 and Rs. 32,018,308k in 2023), the **Operating Profit Margin** works out to approximately **23.5%** in 2024, up from about **18.7%** in 2023.

 

_Figure: Operating Profit Margin (% of revenue) for 2023 and 2024._

 

The chart above illustrates that the operating profit margin improved significantly year-over-year – rising from **18.7% in 2023** to **23.5% in 2024**. This indicates that in 2024 the company converted a much larger share of its revenue into operating profit compared to 2023. The jump in operating margin can be attributed to both **increased operating income** and **effective cost management**. Notably, the 2024 financials show a substantial favorable swing in impairment charges: in 2024 the company recorded a net reversal of impairment (a credit of Rs. 1.16 billion) whereas 2023 had an impairment charge of Rs. 568 million. This reversal effectively boosted the 2024 operating profit. Even though certain operating expenses (like other operating expenses) rose in 2024, the overall operating income grew and expenses were controlled well enough that operating profit increased by 22%. The higher operating margin in 2024 suggests improved operational efficiency – the company was better at managing operating costs (including loan loss provisions and overheads) relative to its income, leading to a stronger core profitability.

## Net Profit Margin

**Definition & Importance:** Net Profit Margin (also called **Net Margin**) is the percentage of revenue that remains as **net profit** after **all expenses** have been deducted, including interest and taxes[investopedia.com](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/p/profitabilityratios.asp#:~:text=The%20gross%20profit%20margin%2C%20operating,reflects%20the%20profit%20from%20the)[investopedia.com](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/p/profitabilityratios.asp#:~:text=Net%20Profit%20Margin). It essentially shows how much of each rupee of revenue the company ultimately keeps as earnings. This is a comprehensive indicator of overall profitability and cost control – a higher net profit margin means the company is more successful at converting revenue into actual profit after covering all costs. It reflects not only operational efficiency but also the impact of financing costs and tax management. Investors and analysts often view net profit margin as a key measure of a firm’s overall financial health[investopedia.com](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/p/profitabilityratios.asp#:~:text=Net%20profit%20margin%20is%20seen,keeping%20all%20costs%20under%20control).

 

**Formula:** Net Profit Margin = (Net Profit / Revenue) × 100%. **Net Profit** (net income) is the profit after all expenses and taxes have been subtracted from revenue[365financialanalyst.com](https://365financialanalyst.com/knowledge-hub/financial-analysis/profitability-ratios/#:~:text=). **Revenue** is total income (gross revenue).

 

**Financial Statement Sources:** For calculation, we use the **Statement of Profit or Loss**. _Net Profit_ is the “Profit for the year” attributable to equity holders of the company (since we are considering the company-only figures) and _Revenue_ is again the gross income from the P&L. The profit for the year is found at the bottom of the income statement, after taxes.

 

**Calculation (2023 vs 2024):** In 2024, People’s Leasing & Finance’s **Net Profit (Company)** was Rs. 3,540,497k, compared to Rs. 3,017,638k in 2023. Using the revenue figures (Rs. 31,036,624k in 2024 and Rs. 32,018,308k in 2023), the **Net Profit Margin** is approximately **11.4%** for 2024 versus **9.4%** for 2023.

 

_Figure: Net Profit Margin (% of revenue) for 2023 and 2024._

 

The net profit margin for People’s Leasing & Finance PLC increased from **9.4% in 2023** to **11.4% in 2024**, as depicted in the figure. This indicates that the company was able to convert a larger portion of its revenue into actual profit in 2024. The rise in net margin aligns with the improvements observed in the gross and operating margins – it signifies that not only did operating performance improve, but the company also managed its interest costs and taxes such that more of the revenue flowed through to the bottom line. In 2024, profit after tax grew by about 17% year-on-year while revenue slightly declined, resulting in a higher net margin. This improvement suggests enhanced overall efficiency and profitability. It may be attributed to factors such as reduced funding costs, effective cost containment, and the one-time gain from impairment reversals, coupled with relatively stable tax expenses (income tax expense did increase in 2024, but the effect was outweighed by the higher pre-tax profit growth). A higher net profit margin in 2024 is a positive sign, indicating stronger overall financial performance and better returns on each unit of revenue compared to 2023.

## Return on Assets (ROA)

**Definition & Importance:** Return on Assets (ROA) measures how effectively a company uses its assets to generate profit. It is the ratio of net profit to total assets, often expressed as a percentage[investopedia.com](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/p/profitabilityratios.asp#:~:text=Profitability%20is%20assessed%20relative%20to,income%20divided%20by%20total%20assets). ROA indicates the profit earned per rupee of assets and thus reflects how efficiently management is deploying the company’s asset base to produce earnings[365financialanalyst.com](https://365financialanalyst.com/knowledge-hub/financial-analysis/profitability-ratios/#:~:text=Return%20on%20assets%20,the%20company%20deploys%20its%20capital). A higher ROA means the company is generating more profit from its assets, signifying efficient use of assets. This ratio is especially relevant for financial institutions like People’s Leasing & Finance, which have large asset bases; it helps investors gauge how well the company’s lending and investment assets are yielding returns.

 

**Formula:** ROA = (Net Profit / Total Assets) × 100%. Typically, net profit is compared to average total assets over the period[365financialanalyst.com](https://365financialanalyst.com/knowledge-hub/financial-analysis/profitability-ratios/#:~:text=We%20use%20the%20following%20formula,calculate%20this%20measure%20of%20profitability). In practice, if average assets are not readily available, year-end total assets can be used as an approximation for a quick analysis. **Net Profit** is the net income for the year (from the income statement), and **Total Assets** is the sum of all assets at the end of the year (from the Statement of Financial Position).

 

**Financial Statement Sources:** We take _Net Profit_ from the **Statement of Profit or Loss** (profit for the year) and _Total Assets_ from the **Statement of Financial Position** (the total assets reported as of 31 March of each year). Here we use the company’s standalone total assets (excluding any group consolidation effects) as given in the balance sheet.

 

**Calculation (2023 vs 2024):** As noted, the company’s net profits were Rs. 3,017,638k in 2023 and Rs. 3,540,497k in 2024. The total assets of the company stood at Rs. 171,073,162k as of 31 March 2023 and Rs. 159,852,088k as of 31 March 2024. Using the year-end asset figures for a simple ROA estimate, **ROA (2023)** = 3,017,638 / 171,073,162 × 100% ≈ **1.76%**, and **ROA (2024)** = 3,540,497 / 159,852,088 × 100% ≈ **2.21%**.

 

_Figure: Return on Assets (ROA) for 2023 and 2024._

 

The ROA for People’s Leasing & Finance PLC improved from about **1.8% in 2023** to **2.2% in 2024**, as illustrated above. This upward movement indicates that the company generated more profit per rupee of assets in 2024 than it did in the previous year. Several factors underlie this improvement: First, **net earnings increased** by 17% in 2024, reflecting higher profitability. Second, the **asset base actually contracted** by roughly 6.6% (total assets fell from ~Rs.171 billion to ~Rs.160 billion), due in part to reduced lending portfolios or other asset adjustments. When a company earns higher profit on a slightly smaller asset base, its ROA naturally rises. The higher ROA in 2024 suggests better efficiency in asset utilization – the assets of People’s Leasing & Finance were put to more productive use, yielding greater returns. This could be due to improved interest spreads, better asset quality (leading to lower impairments), or a more optimal mix of earning assets. In summary, the company’s profitability relative to its assets strengthened in 2024, a positive sign that indicates improved management of assets or operating environment compared to 2023.

## Return on Equity (ROE)

**Definition & Importance:** Return on Equity (ROE) measures the return generated on the owners’ investment in the company. It is defined as net profit divided by shareholders’ equity[investopedia.com](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/p/profitabilityratios.asp#:~:text=Return%20on%20Equity%20). ROE indicates how effectively a company is using shareholders’ funds to create profits. For shareholders, ROE is a critical metric, as it shows the percentage return on each rupee of equity invested in the company[365financialanalyst.com](https://365financialanalyst.com/knowledge-hub/financial-analysis/profitability-ratios/#:~:text=Return%20on%20equity%20,important%20and%20tracked%20profitability%20ratios). A higher ROE means the company is more efficient at converting the equity capital into net income – essentially, it signifies stronger profitability for the owners. It’s often used to compare profitability across companies, and to track a company’s performance over time in generating value for shareholders.

 

**Formula:** ROE = (Net Profit / Shareholders’ Equity) × 100%. Net Profit is the profit after tax (from the income statement) and Shareholders’ Equity is the total equity attributable to the company’s shareholders (from the balance sheet). Like ROA, the net profit is often compared to average equity over the period[365financialanalyst.com](https://365financialanalyst.com/knowledge-hub/financial-analysis/profitability-ratios/#:~:text=Return%20on%20equity%20,important%20and%20tracked%20profitability%20ratios), but here we will use the year-end equity for consistency with available data.

 

**Financial Statement Sources:** The inputs are drawn from two statements: _Net Profit_ from the **Statement of Profit or Loss** (company profit for the year) and _Total Shareholders’ Equity_ from the **Statement of Financial Position** (total equity attributable to equity holders at year-end). We consider only the company’s equity (excluding non-controlling interests, which are only relevant at the group level – in the company-only financials, there is no NCI).

 

**Calculation (2023 vs 2024):** People’s Leasing & Finance PLC’s standalone profit for 2023 was Rs. 3,017,638k and for 2024 was Rs. 3,540,497k. The corresponding total equity (Company’s shareholders’ equity) was Rs. 39,792,065k as of 31 March 2023 and Rs. 41,826,138k as of 31 March 2024. Using these figures, **ROE (2023)** = 3,017,638 / 39,792,065 × 100% ≈ **7.58%**, and **ROE (2024)** = 3,540,497 / 41,826,138 × 100% ≈ **8.46%**.

 

_Figure: Return on Equity (ROE) for 2023 and 2024._

 

The ROE of People’s Leasing & Finance PLC increased from roughly **7.6% in 2023** to **8.5% in 2024**, as shown in the chart. This means that in 2024 the company generated about 8.5 cents of profit for every rupee of equity, compared to about 7.6 cents per rupee in the prior year. The rise in ROE reflects the company’s enhanced ability to deliver returns to its shareholders. Contributing to this improvement was the growth in net profit (17% higher in 2024 than 2023) coupled with only a modest increase in equity (shareholders’ equity grew by about 5% year-over-year due to retained earnings and reserves). Because profit grew at a faster rate than equity, the return on equity rose. This trend is favorable for shareholders, as it indicates that the company is not sitting on underutilized capital; instead, it is leveraging its equity base to produce higher earnings. A ROE of 8.5% (while an improvement) is moderate, suggesting there is still room to further enhance shareholder returns, but the year-over-year gain signals a positive trajectory in how effectively the company is employing equity capital to generate profits.

## Conclusion

In summary, the profitability ratios of People’s Leasing & Finance PLC (Company-only) show a **clear improvement from 2023 to 2024**. The **Gross Profit Margin** rose slightly (from ~43% to ~45%), indicating better control of direct costs (interest costs) relative to income. The **Operating Profit Margin** saw a substantial increase, reflecting enhanced operational efficiency and lower net impairment costs in 2024. Consequently, the **Net Profit Margin** also improved, demonstrating that a greater proportion of revenue is being translated into actual profit after all expenses and taxes. In terms of return metrics, **ROA** increased from about 1.8% to 2.2%, suggesting the company made more effective use of its asset base to generate earnings. Similarly, **ROE** climbed from roughly 7.6% to 8.5%, indicating improved returns for shareholders.

 

Overall, the year-over-year trend points to a strengthening profitability profile for People’s Leasing & Finance PLC in 2024. The company managed to boost profits despite a slight dip in revenue, implying success in cost management and perhaps more prudent risk/asset management (as evidenced by the reduction in impairment charges). These improvements in profitability ratios signal that the company’s financial performance and efficiency have improved, which is a positive sign for stakeholders. Going forward, sustaining or further enhancing these ratios – through revenue growth, continued cost discipline, and efficient use of assets and equity – will be key for the company to continue delivering value to its shareholders and maintaining a healthy financial position.
