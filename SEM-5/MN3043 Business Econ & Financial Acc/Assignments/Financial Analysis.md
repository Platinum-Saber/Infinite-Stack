
# 📊 Profitability Ratio Analysis

*(For the year ended 31st March 2024 vs 2023)*

### 1. **Gross Profit Margin (GPM)**

$$
\text{Gross Profit Margin} = \frac{\text{Gross Profit}}{\text{Revenue}} \times 100
$$

* Gross income (proxy for revenue): Rs. 31,036,624 (2024), Rs. 32,018,308 (2023).
* Interest expense deducted gives Net Interest Income, but since detailed Cost of Sales isn’t explicitly broken out (typical for finance companies), we use **Net Operating Income** as a proxy for Gross Profit.

$$
\text{GPM (2024)} = \frac{14,991,515}{31,036,624} \times 100 \approx 48.3\%
$$

$$
\text{GPM (2023)} = \frac{13,242,880}{32,018,308} \times 100 \approx 41.4\%
$$

✅ **Observation:** Gross profitability improved significantly due to better net interest spreads and reduced impairments.

---

### 2. **Operating Profit Margin (OPM)**

$$
\text{Operating Profit Margin} = \frac{\text{Operating Profit}}{\text{Revenue}} \times 100
$$

* Operating profit before taxes on financial services = Rs. 7,296,856 (2024), Rs. 5,973,224 (2023).

$$
\text{OPM (2024)} = \frac{7,296,856}{31,036,624} \times 100 \approx 23.5\%
$$

$$
\text{OPM (2023)} = \frac{5,973,224}{32,018,308} \times 100 \approx 18.7\%
$$

✅ **Observation:** Operating efficiency strengthened; the company converted a higher share of revenue into operating profit.

---

### 3. **Net Profit Margin (NPM)**

$$
\text{Net Profit Margin} = \frac{\text{Net Profit}}{\text{Revenue}} \times 100
$$

* Net Profit after tax = Rs. 3,540,497 (2024), Rs. 3,017,638 (2023).

$$
\text{NPM (2024)} = \frac{3,540,497}{31,036,624} \times 100 \approx 11.4\%
$$

$$
\text{NPM (2023)} = \frac{3,017,638}{32,018,308} \times 100 \approx 9.4\%
$$

✅ **Observation:** Net margin improved by \~2%, reflecting stronger cost control and reduced impairment losses.

---

### 4. **Return on Assets (ROA)**

$$
\text{ROA} = \frac{\text{Net Profit}}{\text{Average Total Assets}} \times 100
$$

* Total assets = Rs. 159,852,088 (2024), Rs. 171,073,162 (2023).
* Average assets = (159,852,088 + 171,073,162) ÷ 2 = **165,462,625**.

$$
\text{ROA (2024)} = \frac{3,540,497}{165,462,625} \times 100 \approx 2.1\%
$$

$$
\text{ROA (2023)} = \frac{3,017,638}{171,073,162} \times 100 \approx 1.8\%
$$

✅ **Observation:** Asset profitability increased, though still modest compared to non-financial firms (expected for finance sector).

---

### 5. **Return on Equity (ROE)**

$$
\text{ROE} = \frac{\text{Net Profit}}{\text{Average Equity}} \times 100
$$

* Equity = Rs. 41,826,138 (2024), Rs. 39,792,065 (2023).
* Average equity = (41,826,138 + 39,792,065) ÷ 2 = **40,809,102**.

$$
\text{ROE (2024)} = \frac{3,540,497}{40,809,102} \times 100 \approx 8.7\%
$$

$$
\text{ROE (2023)} = \frac{3,017,638}{39,792,065} \times 100 \approx 7.6\%
$$

✅ **Observation:** ROE improved by \~1.1%, showing stronger shareholder returns.

---

| ROE                     | 7.6%  | 8.7%  | ↑ Improved |

---

### 🔎 Final Insights

* Profitability ratios all show **year-on-year improvement**, driven mainly by:

  * Lower impairment charges on loans.
  * Controlled operating expenses despite revenue decline.
* The company’s **net margin** and **ROE** are growing, but still moderate, reflecting the capital-intensive and regulated nature of the finance sector.
* Investors and analysts would view this as a **positive recovery trend** despite slightly lower revenues.
